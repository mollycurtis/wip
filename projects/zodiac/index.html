    A birth chart is a snapshot of the sky the moment you were born.

    It only takes three ideas to read one.

    Planets are the what

    Each one stands for a part of you. Venus is how you love, Mars is your drive, Mercury is how you think.

    Signs are the how

    The sign a planet sits in gives it a style. Venus in a bold sign loves boldly. Venus in a careful sign loves carefully.

    Houses are the where

    The chart is split into 12 areas of life, like home, career and friends. A planet's house is where its energy shows up.
